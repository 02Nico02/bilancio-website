## Brand & Style
The design system positions personal wealth management as an act of deliberate, quiet reflection rather than frantic speculation or mechanical bookkeeping. It rejects the hyperactive neon palettes of day-trading crypto platforms and the bureaucratic clutter of legacy retail banking in favor of an editorial atmosphere reminiscent of a bespoke Swiss private ledger. 

The aesthetic is grounded in architectural minimalism paired with classical publishing nuance: tactile paper-like canvas tones, high-discipline grid alignments, hairline structural borders, and generous breathing room. Visual hierarchies evoke calm mastery, clarity of thought, and timeless financial stewardship. Every component serves to de-escalate financial anxiety, replacing high-stress alerts with composed, dignifying typography and understated accents.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on an organic, warm-stone base that eliminates glare and sterile digital white:
- **Canvas & Surface System:** The global canvas rests on a soft, warm limestone (`#FBFBF9`). Component containers and interactive cards lift into crisp, luminous chalk (`#FFFFFF`), delimited by precise hairline borders (`#EAEAE4`). Muted tertiary containers use soft ecru (`#F4F4EE`).
- **Deep Ink Hierarchy:** Text avoids harsh pure blacks in favor of deep vegetal ink:
  - High-emphasis text: Deep Ink (`#1A1A18`)
  - Medium-emphasis text: Weathered Slate (`#6E6E66`)
  - Low-emphasis & captions: Muted Pebble (`#9C9C94`)
- **Accents & Financial Semantics:**
  - **Inflows / Net Worth / Primary Accent:** Deep Forest Juniper (`#1B4332`). Authoritative, organic, and reassuring.
  - **Outflows / Expenses:** Terracotta Rust (`#A8422B`). Clearly signaling debits without invoking panic or neon-red visual fatigue.
  - **Asset Allocation & Categories:** Muted ochre (`#D4A373`), dusty slate (`#4A5568`), and sage grey (`#87986A`) provide non-competing structural color coding.

## Typography
Typography is the cornerstone of this design system's identity, pairing the literary dignity of `Newsreader` with the disciplined functional clarity of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Editorial Headlines & Significant Balances:** Total net worth, key portfolio totals, and high-level section statements are rendered in `Newsreader`. This imparts an institutional, enduring character. 
- **Tabular Rigor & Numeric Layout:** All monetary figures rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` must employ OpenType feature settings `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv01" 1` to guarantee rigid column alignment across data grids.
- **Micro-Currency Detailing:** When displaying granular balances, minor decimals (cents) are formatted at 80% opacity and 1px smaller scale to ensure instantaneous scanning of top-level integer magnitudes without visual distraction.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined 12-column architectural grid with structural margins and generous vertical padding. Whitespace is treated as an active layout material rather than vacant space.

- **Breakpoints & Layout Structure:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid, max-width 1360px centered, 2.5rem (`space-xl`) outer margins, 1.5rem (`gutter`) column gaps.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, 1.5rem outer margins, 1rem gutters. Sidebars fold into an understated drawer or floating bottom sheet.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, 1rem outer margins, 0.75rem gutters. Vertical rhythm prioritizes single-column linear flows.
- **Rhythmic Densities:** Data tables and statement lists adopt a "comfortable density" standard: table rows feature 1rem vertical padding, creating breathing room while preserving high informational utility.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids theatrical drop shadows, skeuomorphic bevels, or heavy frosted glass. Depth is communicated strictly through surface layering and razor-thin architectural boundaries:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** `#FBFBF9` establishes the grounding layer.
  - **Tier 1 (Cards, Modules, Sidebars):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px solid border (`#EAEAE4`).
  - **Tier 2 (Popovers, Flyouts, Tooltips):** `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#DDDCD4`) and an ultra-diffused, ambient ground shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(26, 26, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Ghost Outlines:** Instead of heavy bounding lines, separators between list items and inner panels use an understated 1px rule (`#F0F0EA`), keeping structural lines light and crisp.

## Shapes
Geometry is disciplined, crisp, and understated. The shape language adheres to a subtle radius strategy (`roundedness: 1`):
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** 0.5rem (`rounded-lg`) corner radius, maintaining structural alignment with the grid without appearing rigid or blunt.
- **Buttons & Form Inputs:** 0.375rem corner radius, providing crisp tactile borders.
- **Status Tags & Micro-Pills:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) reserved strictly for categorical classification tags and trend badges, creating an intentional contrast against the architectural cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Forest Juniper (`#1B4332`) solid background, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle 0.375rem radius. Hover shifts to an understated deep shade (`#143527`) with a 150ms smooth transition.
- **Secondary:** Chalk white (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 1px hairline border (`#D8D8CF`), `#1A1A18` text. Hover subtle wash (`#F6F6F2`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border, transparent background, `#6E6E66` text, active underline or tint on hover.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, a 1px border in `#EAEAE4`, and 0.5rem radius.
- Padding inside cards adheres strictly to `space-lg` (1.5rem) on desktop and `space-md` (1rem) on mobile. Header dividers inside cards are set to hairline `#F2F2EC`.

### Input Fields
- Background is crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a 1px border (`#DCDCD6`).
- Placeholder text in `#9C9C94`.
- Focus state eschews thick rings; it uses a 1px primary border (`#1B4332`) accompanied by a 1px outline offset in Juniper tint (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.12)`).

### Transaction Lists & Data Tables
- Header labels are set in `label-sm`, uppercase with 0.06em tracking in `#6E6E66`.
- Rows feature a 1px bottom border in `#F4F4EE` and 1rem vertical padding.
- Numerical values are strictly right-aligned with `tnum` font styling. 
- Inflow amounts feature Juniper (`#1B4332`) with a subtle `+` prefix; debits display `#1A1A18` text (or `#A8422B` when indicating budget overruns or deficit states).

### Badges & Category Chips
- Compact micro-pills with height 22px, pill-radii (`9999px`), padding `0 0.5rem`.
- Set with low-saturation backgrounds (e.g., `#EBF2ED` for positive/savings, `#F7ECE9` for outflows/expenses) with corresponding high-contrast foreground typography.

### Financial Visualizations
- **Trend Charts:** Hairline 1.5px paths in `#1B4332` over a faint vertical gradient fade (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.04)` to transparent). Minimal dotted horizontal reference lines in `#EAEAE4`.
- **Allocation Strips:** Clean, continuous segmented progress bar with 4px height and 2px segment spacing, utilizing the muted category palette without high-contrast neon borders.
